**BLINK 1.4.0 shows Claude Enterprise spend limits.** An Enterprise login is metered by a dollar cap rather than by a five-hour window, and until now the Claude page had no way to show one. It also handles switching between a Max login and a corporate one, which is how this release was asked for.

## What changed

- **A spend page for Enterprise.** When Claude Code reports a spend cap (it does behind a Claude apps gateway), the Claude page shows the share of the cap used and when it resets on the left, and the dollars spent against the cap on the right. Near the cap it says "Spend limit almost used up".
- **"Not reported" instead of blank gauges.** Signed in directly to an Enterprise or Team account, Claude Code reports no limits, and nothing on the computer has the figure. The page now says so rather than showing dashes that look like a fault.
- **Switching accounts is safe.** BLINK notices when `/login` changes the account and ignores every reading taken before the switch, so your Max session numbers never appear under the corporate account, or the other way round. It reads which kind of account is signed in from Claude Code's profile in `~/.claude.json`; it never reads the login token.

## Updating

Run **`blink update`** to install this version, then the screen offers the firmware and one tap installs it. The spend page needs both halves: until the board is updated, an Enterprise spend figure shows as dashes rather than under the wrong name.

Automatic app updates are off for this release, so nothing installs on its own.

Something wrong? Open an issue at **[github.com/gapietro/AIFBlink/issues](https://github.com/gapietro/AIFBlink/issues)** with the output of `blink status` and the last lines of `bridge.log`.
