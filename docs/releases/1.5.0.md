**BLINK 1.5.0 is themed for the AI Foundry team.** The board's screens take their colours from the AIF logo, and the header reads AIF BLINK. Nothing about the numbers changes.

## What changed

- **AI Foundry colours.** The gauge, settings and setup screens use the AIF navy as their background and the AIF green for accents. The green, amber and red that warn you about a limit are unchanged, and were re-checked for contrast against the new background.
- **AIF BLINK in the header**, in the AIF green.
- **An AIF startup screen**, for boards that are re-flashed with it: the AIF mark with "AI Foundry" under it, after the boot animation. It goes on over the USB cable (`tools/make_aif_startup.py`, then `tools/burn.sh --logo`), not through an update.

## Updating

Run **`blink update`** to install this version, then the screen offers the firmware and one tap installs it. The colours and header arrive with the firmware.

Automatic app updates are off for this release, so nothing installs on its own.

Something wrong? Open an issue at **[github.com/gapietro/AIFBlink/issues](https://github.com/gapietro/AIFBlink/issues)** with the output of `blink status` and the last lines of `bridge.log`.
