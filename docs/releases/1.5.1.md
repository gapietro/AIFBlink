**BLINK 1.5.1 makes the AI Foundry theme easier to read.** On the board's panel, 1.5.0's navy looked washed out, so the background is now a deeper navy, and the settings screen has more contrast.

## What changed

- **A darker background on every screen.** The same navy hue, much deeper. Everything on it reads more clearly, including the green, amber and red that warn you about a limit.
- **More contrast on the settings screen.** White text, brighter secondary text, and cards that stand out more from the background.
- **The AIF startup screen matches.** Boards that carry it should be re-flashed with the new one (`tools/make_aif_startup.py`, then `tools/burn.sh --logo dist/aif-startup.png`), or it will show the old, lighter navy before the gauges.

## Updating

Run **`blink update`** to install this version, then the screen offers the firmware and one tap installs it.

Automatic app updates are off for this release, so nothing installs on its own.

Something wrong? Open an issue at **[github.com/gapietro/AIFBlink/issues](https://github.com/gapietro/AIFBlink/issues)** with the output of `blink status` and the last lines of `bridge.log`.
