**BLINK 1.6.0 adds a contrast setting.** On a panel that looks washed out, the screen's contrast can now be turned up from the board itself.

## What changed

- **Settings -> Display.** The Brightness setting is now Display: the five brightness levels, and below them the screen contrast, **Normal**, **High** or **Max**. The change shows the moment you tap, and is kept across restarts.
- **Normal is what you had.** An updated board looks exactly as it did until you choose. **High** is the usual cure for this panel looking flat; **Max** goes a step further for deeper blacks.

## Updating

Run **`blink update`** to install this version, then the screen offers the firmware and one tap installs it. Your brightness and other settings carry over.

Automatic app updates are off for this release, so nothing installs on its own.

Something wrong? Open an issue at **[github.com/gapietro/AIFBlink/issues](https://github.com/gapietro/AIFBlink/issues)** with the output of `blink status` and the last lines of `bridge.log`.
