**BLINK 1.7.0 adds badges.** As you use the board, you earn badges for goals met along the way, and Settings -> Badges shows the collection.

## What changed

- **Fifteen badges.** Ten everyday ones -- First Light, Early Bird, Night Owl, Multitasker, Agent Wrangler, Polyglot, Week Streak, Quick Draw, Cool Head and Centurion -- three hard ones that take weeks, and two hidden ones that show as "???" until you find them.
- **Settings -> Badges** shows them in a grid: earned ones in colour, the rest dimmed. Tap one to see what earns it.
- **A new badge is announced** on the gauges: "Badge earned: Night Owl".
- **Each computer keeps its own collection,** in `~/.blink/badges.json`, so it survives a board swap or an update.
- **Settings has three rows now:** Display, Badges and Software update. The version numbers are under Software update.

None of the badges reward using up your allowance for its own sake; a few reward the opposite.

## Updating

Run **`blink update`** to install this version, then the screen offers the firmware and one tap installs it. Both halves are needed: the app earns the badges and the board shows them.

Automatic app updates are off for this release, so nothing installs on its own.

Something wrong? Open an issue at **[github.com/gapietro/AIFBlink/issues](https://github.com/gapietro/AIFBlink/issues)** with the output of `blink status` and the last lines of `bridge.log`.
